Our Story

Thirty Years,
One Standard

We opened on Alvarado Boulevard in 1992 with one case of mithai and a tandoor. The Union City shop is still home — and our catering kitchen travels the length of Northern California.

The rule hasn't changed: if it wouldn't go on our own table, it doesn't go in the case. Sweets are made in small batches through the day, so the barfi you buy at 6pm hasn't been sitting since dawn.

Before You Ask

Good Questions

How far ahead should I book catering?

For a wedding, as early as you can — good dates go a year out. For parties under a hundred, two to three weeks is usually comfortable. If it's this weekend, call rather than email; we can often still make it work.

Do you travel outside the Bay Area?

Yes. We cater across Northern California and have done jobs in Sacramento, Modesto and further. Travel gets added to the quote once we know the venue.

Can you do a fully vegetarian or Jain menu?

A full vegetarian menu is standard for us — most of the case is vegetarian anyway. Jain, no-onion-no-garlic and vegan menus are all doable; mention it when you call so we plan the kitchen around it.

Is there a minimum order for sweets?

No minimum at the counter — buy one piece if you like. For decorated gift boxes and bulk festival orders, give us a few days, especially around Diwali and Rakhi.

Do you set up the buffet, or just drop the food off?

Either. Drop-off means hot food in chafing dishes at your door. Full service means our staff arrive early, set the stalls, serve through the event and clear afterwards.

Can we taste the food before booking?

For weddings and larger events, yes — we'll arrange a tasting at the Union City shop. For smaller jobs, come eat at the counter; it's the same kitchen.
